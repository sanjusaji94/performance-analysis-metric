### Q2: Which of these programs are CPU-bound?

Answer:
To determine whether the programs are CPU-bound, I compared their CPU time (User + System)
with their total real-world runtime (Elapsed), and the relationship in the "Percent of CPU this job got" value reported by the 'time' tool.

A program is CPU-bound when the CPU is actively executing code for most of the 
runtime (User time + System time equals the Elapsed), and I/O-bound when it spends much of its time waiting for
external operations (Elapsed is greater than CPU time). And in the case of Percent of CPU a program got, a CPU-bound program will 
show a utilization close to 100% or above 100% if it uses multiple cores ,whereas an I/O-bound or idle program will have a much lower CPU percentage.
